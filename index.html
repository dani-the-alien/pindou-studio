Open in another tab

Only one tab edits at a time, so two tabs can't overwrite each other's work. When the other tab is closed, a visible paused tab carries on by itself.

Share as a link

Anyone who opens this link gets their own copy of this design in Pindou Studio. The design is inside the link itself; Pindou Studio doesn't upload it anywhere.

To bookmark the design: turn this on, then bookmark the page (Ctrl+D, ⌘D, or the ☆ in the address bar or browser menu). While it's on, the address follows your edits in this tab, so each bookmark saves the design as it was when you made it.
